The analyst's review

Norway edged a five-goal contest 3-2 at MetLife Stadium, a result that blew straight past the model's expectation of a cagey, low-scoring evening. The numbers had this as a coin-flip game with the away side marginally favoured — Senegal at 37.2% to Norway's 33.4%, with a 29.4% draw — and the most likely scorelines were all tight: 1-1 at 13.8%, then 0-1, 0-0 and 1-0 clustered around 10%. Five goals was firmly against the grain, with over 4.5 priced at just 8.9% and even over 3.5 only a 20.9% shot. So the goal glut itself was a clear miss for the model.

On the result, though, the verdict is kinder. Polymarket leaned Norway at 44.5 cents, well ahead of the model's home number, and the market read the outcome better here. Our locked bracket pick landed on Norway too, calling the home win correctly even if the projected 1-1 scoreline never materialised — a hit on result if not on margin. A night where the market's faith in Norway paid off, and the goal model got the texture of the game wrong.
