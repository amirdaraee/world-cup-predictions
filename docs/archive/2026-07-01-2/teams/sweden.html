The analyst's notes

Sweden arrive ranked 38th in the world, a UEFA side with a squad valued at just over €406m built around a defensive spine — Lindelöf, Hien, Lagerbielke and Ekdal — and a goalkeeping rotation of Johansson, Nordfeldt and Widell Zetterström. The model treats this as a side likely to make the cut but not much more.
