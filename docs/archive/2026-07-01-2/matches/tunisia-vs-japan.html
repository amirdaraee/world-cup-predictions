The analyst's review

Japan brushed Tunisia aside 0-4 at the Estadio BBVA, a result that was the right outcome but a heavier scoreline than anyone forecast. The away win itself was well flagged: our model made Japan 62.6% favourites and Polymarket priced them at 56.5 cents, with a Tunisia win a remote 12.1%. The locked bracket pick of an away win duly landed. Japan's quality, reflected in a squad valued at €270.85m against Tunisia's €69.95m, told emphatically once the goals started flowing.

Where everyone underplayed it was the margin. The model's 1.61 expected goals for Japan pointed to a controlled, narrow win — the single most likely scoreline was 0-1 at 17.9%, with 0-2 next at 14.8%. A four-goal haul sat far out in the tail: over 3.5 goals was just 17.4% and over 4.5 only 6.9%. Tunisia, fresh off a 0-5 friendly loss to Belgium, again offered nothing going forward, and the clean sheet against them matched the script even as the avalanche at the other end did not. Right call on the winner, well short on the scale of it.
