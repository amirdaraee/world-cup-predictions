The analyst's review

Canada turned a crowd at BC Place into a procession, hammering Qatar 6-0 in Vancouver. The scoreline blew past anything either the model or the market had on its radar: a six-goal margin sat in the tail, with our model giving over 5.5 goals just an 18.3% chance and topping out its likeliest scores at a tidy 3-0 (12.1%) or 2-0 (11.4%). The shape of the night, though, was exactly as advertised. Canada were always the heavy favourite, and they played like it.

On the headline question the model read it well, pricing Canada at 86.3% to win against Polymarket's more cautious 75.5c — and the result vindicated the higher number emphatically. The 3.18 expected goals for the hosts pointed to a comfortable evening; six was simply the high end of that distribution arriving all at once. Qatar, who hadn't scored in their last three outings coming in, never threatened the 15.8% first-to-score chance the model afforded them. Our locked bracket pick of 2-0 Canada nailed the result if not the rout. A clean call on direction, beaten only on magnitude.

The analyst's preview

Vancouver hosts a clear mismatch on paper. Canada sit 30th in the world to Qatar's 55th, and the gap in squad value is stark — €198.65m against just €19.93m. The model reads it as a near-rout: 3.18 expected goals for Canada against 0.61, a home win priced at 86.3% with the draw down at 9.8% and a Qatar win a remote 4.0%. There's logic behind it. Qatar arrive in poor form, beaten by Tunisia 3-0 and Palestine in the Arab Cup and unable to score in three of their last five, while Canada are unbeaten in their last five at home. Expect Canada to set the tone early — they're 81.7% to open the scoring and 66.5% to lead at the break.

Where the model and market part ways is on degree, not direction. Polymarket also makes Canada heavy favourites but trims them to 76.5%, paying more respect to the draw at 16.5 cents. The bigger split is on goals: the model loves overs, pricing over 2.5 at 73.0% against the market's 56.5%, and over 1.5 at 89.4% versus 79.5%. If you trust the xG, Canada by three or more is the live outcome; the market is hedging that conviction.
