How to read this: a probability model lives or dies on its log-loss against the market, not on how often its single likeliest pick lands. Raw "results called" sinks whenever draws cluster — a draw is rarely any one match's most-likely score — so the headline below leads with the model's log-loss and how it sits versus the market.
