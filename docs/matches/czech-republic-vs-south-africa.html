The model reads it as a controlled rather than commanding edge, projecting 1.32 expected goals to 0.85 and pricing the Czechs at 47.2% to win, with the draw a live 29.6% and South Africa 23.3%. This profiles as a tight, low-scoring affair — over 2.5 goals is just a 36.9% shot, both teams to score only 42.6%, and the single likeliest outcomes are a 1-0 (14.5%), a 1-1 (13.4%) and a goalless draw (12.1%). Notably, the model has the half-time draw as favourite at 47.7%, suggesting a slow start.

The market is a touch more bullish on the Czechs, pricing them at 51.5 cents against the model's 47, and meaningfully more confident on goals: Polymarket has over 2.5 at 45.5 cents and both teams to score at 49.5, well clear of the model's reads. That gap on totals is the genuine open question here — the numbers say cagey, the market expects a more open game.
