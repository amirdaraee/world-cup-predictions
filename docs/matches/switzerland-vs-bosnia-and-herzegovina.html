The analyst's review

Switzerland made their favouritism count at SoFi Stadium, brushing Bosnia and Herzegovina aside 4-1 to open Group B with a statement. The scale of it outstripped expectations: five goals cleared the over-4.5 line the model gave just a 13.5% chance, and Switzerland's four alone beat their 2.06 expected-goals projection. Bosnia's consolation kept both teams on the scoresheet, landing the both-teams-to-score outcome priced at 41%, but it never threatened the result.

On the big question the model was emphatic and correct: it made Switzerland 70.6% to win against just 9.7% for Bosnia, and the locked bracket pick of a 1-0 home win nailed the result if not the lavish margin. Where the read missed was the goal glut — a projected 50% chance of over 2.5 understated a contest that blew past 4.5 — and the expected one-sidedness of the scoreline proved generous to Switzerland, who outdid their xG comfortably. Call it a clean win on direction and a humbling on totals: the favourite was right, but the model badly under-counted the goals.
