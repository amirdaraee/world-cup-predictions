The analyst's review

The clean sheet was no surprise — the model gave Algeria just a 38.4% chance of scoring and pegged their attack at 0.62 expected goals — but the three-goal margin pushed past the central forecast. So the result direction was nailed; the scoring volume ran a touch hot.

The model made Argentina 64.0% to win, Polymarket a firmer 69.5 cents, and the favourite duly delivered without fuss. Algeria's 10.5-to-12.2% upset window never opened. That tilt shows up everywhere downstream — Argentina are 66% to score first, the likeliest scorelines are 1-0 (16%) and 2-0 (14%), and both teams to score is only a 38% shot. Where the model and market part company is mild but consistent. Polymarket prices Argentina a touch shorter at 68.5 cents against the model's 64%, and leans slightly more toward goals, putting over 2.5 at 47.5 cents versus the model's 41.8% and BTTS at 41.5 against 38.4%.
