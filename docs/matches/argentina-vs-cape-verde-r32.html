Five goals cleared the over 4.5 line the model priced at only 18.2%, and both teams scoring landed despite a BTTS probability of just 25.2%.

On the headline, the model and market were vindicated: an 86.7% home win came home, and the 2.67 expected goals for Argentina squared neatly with three scored. Where both views missed was Cape Verde's attack. The model had them at 0.31 xG and a 9.8% chance of scoring first, and its five most likely scorelines were all Argentina clean sheets, topped by 2-0 at 18.1%. Two goals from a side the projection barely credited is a clear miss on the defensive read, even if the result itself was never really in doubt. Good call on the winner, poor call on how comfortable it would be.
