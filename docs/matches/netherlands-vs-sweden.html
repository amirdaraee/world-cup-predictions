The analyst's review

Netherlands brushed Sweden aside 5-1 at the NRG Stadium, a result that overshot even the rosiest pre-match read. The Oranje had been pegged at a 63.1% win by the model and 59.5 cents on Polymarket, so the favourite delivering was no surprise — but the manner of it was. The expected goals line of 2.1 to 0.96 pointed to a comfortable Dutch edge, not a six-goal rout, and the scoreline blew past the totals curve: over 4.5 goals was priced at just 19.5% and over 5.5 at a mere 9%. Sweden, who arrived on a rough run, at least avoided the clean sheet the model gave a strong chance of denying them.

Grading it honestly, the headline call was right and the bracket pick banked it — Netherlands as the winner landed cleanly, even if the locked 1-1 scoreline never resembled the final tally. Where both model and market fell short was magnitude: a 58.97% over-2.5 read captured a high-scoring lean, but nobody saw five Dutch goals coming. A correct result, then, undersold by every number we ran.

The analyst's preview

This is a clear favourite against a side that arrives out of form. Netherlands sit seventh in the world to Sweden's 38th, with a squad valued at €754.2m against €406.1m, and the model reflects that gap: a projected 2.1 to 0.96 in expected goals and a 63% home win, against 21% for the draw and just 16% for Sweden. The reasoning is straightforward — the Dutch have lost only once in their last ten and built a qualifying run on heavy home wins, while Sweden limped through theirs with a string of defeats to Kosovo, Switzerland and others before steadier recent results. The model has Netherlands first to score 65% of the time and tips 2-0, 2-1 and 1-1 as the likeliest scorelines.

The market broadly agrees but trims the favourite. Polymarket prices Netherlands at 57.5 cents to the model's 63%, nudging both the draw (23.5 against 21%) and a Sweden upset (19.5 against 16%) a little higher — a modest vote for variance in a one-off group game. The sharper split is on both teams scoring: the model sees 55%, the market 60.5 cents, suggesting punters expect Sweden to find the net more readily than the numbers do. On totals the two are almost identical, with over 2.5 around 59%.
