The analyst's review

Portugal turned a heavy favourite's billing into a rout, sweeping aside Uzbekistan 5-0 in Houston. The fifth-ranked side simply overwhelmed opponents 45 places below them, and a clean sheet underlined the gulf that the €1.01bn-to-€85m squad-value chasm always implied. The locked bracket pick of 1-0 to Portugal landed on result — home win — even if the scale of the win blew past it.

Both the model and the market had the right favourite, and the market had the firmer grip: Polymarket's 78.5 cents on Portugal looked closer to the truth than the model's more cautious 66.4%, while Uzbekistan's 7.5-cent and 10.9% away prices were correctly dismissive. Where everyone missed was the goal flood. The model's expected 1.8-0.58 scoreline pointed to something tight, with 1-0 (16.2%) and 2-0 (15.0%) the most likely outcomes and a 0-0 still carrying 9.7%. A five-goal Portugal haul sat firmly in the tail — over 4.5 goals was priced at just 9.4% and over 5.5 at 3.5%. Right call on the winner, but the margin was an outlier neither view saw coming.
