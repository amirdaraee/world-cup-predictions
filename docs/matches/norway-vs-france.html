The analyst's review

France made their favouritism count at Gillette Stadium, running out 4-1 winners over Norway in a game that turned into a far heavier defeat than the numbers had braced for. The world's top-ranked side, backed by a squad valued at €1,520m against Norway's €589.9m, scored the four goals their attacking edge always threatened, and Norway's single reply was little consolation against a contest that ran away from them.

Credit where it's due: both the model and Polymarket leaned firmly France, the model giving them 54.1% to win against 20.6% for Norway, the market almost identical at 53.5% to 22%. That call landed, and the bracket pick of an away win hit too, even if the projected 1-1 scoreline was nowhere near. Where everyone missed was the margin. The model's xG split of 1.69 to 0.95 pointed to a tight, low-scoring night — over 2.5 goals was rated barely a coin flip at 49.3%, and a five-goal haul sat away out at the 12.9% over 4.5 line. France blew through that ceiling. Right result, right favourite, badly underestimated emphasis.

No edge worth chasing; just agreement.
