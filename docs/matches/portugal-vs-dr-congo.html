The analyst's review

Portugal were held 1-1 by DR Congo in Houston, a result that dented the heavy pre-match consensus. Both models leaned firmly on the world's fifth-ranked side: our Dixon-Coles run gave Portugal a 65.8% win and projected a 1.68-to-0.51 xG edge, while Polymarket priced them even shorter at 77 cents. The draw was the underdog's path all along — 24% from the model, just 16.5% on the market — and DR Congo, who arrived off a goalless draw with Denmark, found it. Tellingly, both sides scoring was only a 32.9% shot, yet that's exactly what landed.

So the favourites' backers missed, and our locked bracket pick of 1-0 Portugal went down with them. The honest grade: the model read the shape of the game well, just not the finish. A 1-1 was its fourth-likeliest scoreline at 10%, so this wasn't a freak outcome the numbers ignored — it was a live tail that came in. Credit DR Congo for converting the 10.3% away-or-draw territory the market had all but written off at 75 cents.
