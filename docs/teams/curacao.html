The analyst's notes

Curaçao arrive as the smallest nation ever to reach a World Cup in spirit if not in formal record, ranked 82nd and carrying a squad valued at just €25.78m — a fraction of the resources most of their group rivals can call on. The route here was earned the hard way: across their CONCACAF qualifying matches they never lost, beating Jamaica 2-0 at home, hammering Bermuda 7-0 away, and holding Trinidad and Tobago and Jamaica to draws when it mattered. That is a defensively stubborn, occasionally explosive side built around Dutch-schooled names like Bazoer, Brenet and Floranus.

The warm-up form is the worry. After dispatching Aruba 4-0, Curaçao were beaten heavily on the road — 1-4 by Scotland, 1-5 by Australia, 0-2 by China — against opposition closer to the level they will now face. The model reflects that gap: it gives them an 8.3% shot at surviving the group into the round of 32, just 0.9% to reach the last 16, and effectively nothing beyond. Reaching the tournament at all is the achievement; anything past the group stage would be a genuine upset.
