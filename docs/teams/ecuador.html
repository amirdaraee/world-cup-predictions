The analyst's notes

Ecuador arrive ranked 23rd in the world and unbeaten across their last ten, a run that says plenty about how hard they are to break down. Six of those games were draws, but the wins carry weight: a 1-0 over Argentina in World Cup qualifying, plus friendly defeats of Guatemala (3-0), Saudi Arabia and New Zealand, and away stalemates with the Netherlands, Morocco, Mexico and the USA. The defensive spine is the story — Pacho, Hincapié, Estupiñán and Ordoñez anchor a €368.7m squad that has conceded sparingly, holding both Paraguay and Canada to 0-0.

The model likes the floor more than the ceiling. It makes La Tricolor an 89.7% bet to clear the group stage and gives them a 28.2% chance of winning the group outright, with the round of 16 close to a coin flip at 49.1%. Beyond that the odds thin quickly: 21.7% to reach the quarters, under 10% for the semis, and just 1.4% to lift the trophy. This is a side built to grind and frustrate rather than overwhelm — capable of an awkward run, but a long shot to go all the way.
